# 💍 Hantaran Kahwin & Wedding Styling Services

A sleek, elegant landing page and portfolio showcasing bespoke **Hantaran Kahwin**, **Dulang Cantik**, **Gubahan Eksklusif**, and fresh floral arrangements for weddings and solemnization (nikah) ceremonies. 

Serving clients across **Bangi**, **Kajang**, and **Semenyih**.

---

## ✨ Features & Sections

* **Hero / Intro:** Elegant typography and welcoming visuals introducing your bespoke wedding styling services.
* **Marquee Location Bar:** A smooth, infinite-scrolling ticker highlighting service availability in **Bangi ✦ Kajang ✦ Semenyih**.
* **Services / Gallery Showcase:** Dedicated displays for your signature items:
  * ✦ Frame Mas Kahwin
  * ✦ Custom Themes
  * ✦ Dulang Cantik
  * ✦ Gubahan Eksklusif
  * ✦ Fresh Flowers & Botanical Styling
* **Contact & Booking Section:** Direct inquiry forms or messaging links for brides and grooms to secure their dates.

---

## 🎨 Design & Aesthetic

* **Color Palette:** Rich, warm dark brown (`#352924`) paired with soft, inviting cream text (`#f7f3ef`) for a luxurious, premium feel.
* **Typography:** Classic serif styling paired with minimalist divider accents (`✦`) to evoke timeless elegance suited for Malay wedding traditions.
* **Animations:** Seamless CSS-driven infinite marquee ticker with hover-pause functionality.
